🌿 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding

This technique brings you back to the present moment. Go slowly through each step.

5
THINGS YOU CAN SEE

Look around. Name 5 things you can see right now — a chair, a window, your hands…

4
THINGS YOU CAN TOUCH

Notice 4 physical sensations — the chair beneath you, the floor under your feet…

3
THINGS YOU CAN HEAR

Listen carefully. Name 3 sounds — distant traffic, your own breathing, a fan…

2
THINGS YOU CAN SMELL

Take a breath. Notice 2 scents — the air, your clothes, something nearby…

1
THING YOU CAN TASTE

Notice 1 taste in your mouth right now.

Take your time. There's no rush. Breathe between each step.
